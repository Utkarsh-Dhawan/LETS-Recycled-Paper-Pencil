# KPIs & Success Metrics

## 1. Why Metrics Matter

For LETS, success cannot be measured only by the number of pencils produced or sold.

The product needs to balance:

**Product Quality + Cost Efficiency + Customer Adoption + Business Viability**

The KPI framework therefore tracks the product from prototype validation through commercial scale-up.

---

## 2. North Star Product Objective

> **Make LETS a cost-effective sustainable alternative to conventional pencils without compromising everyday functionality.**

---

## 3. Product Quality KPIs

### Writing Performance

**Objective:** Maintain writing performance comparable to a conventional pencil.

**Metric:**

* User-rated writing quality
* Product complaints related to writing

**Target:** Comparable performance to conventional pencils.

---

### Sharpening Compatibility

**Objective:** Ensure customers can use existing pencil sharpeners.

**Metric:**

* Percentage of tested pencils successfully sharpened using standard sharpeners.

**Current prototype result:** Successfully sharpened using a regular sharpener.

---

### Durability

**Objective:** Ensure the recycled-paper body can withstand normal usage.

**Metrics:**

* Breakage rate
* Lead-break frequency
* Body damage during use

**Target:** Comparable everyday durability to the intended conventional-pencil benchmark.

---

## 4. Cost KPIs

### Production Cost

**Current prototype cost:**

> **₹1.50 / pencil**

**Primary objective:**

> Maintain or reduce unit production cost through process standardization and mechanization.

---

### Manufacturing Efficiency

Track:

* Production time per pencil
* Output per production cycle
* Material wastage
* Defect rate
* Labour hours per batch

These metrics become increasingly important during scale-up.

---

## 5. Business KPIs

### Gross Margin

At the current proposed wholesale price:

**Production cost:** ₹1.50

**Wholesale price:** ₹3.00

**Gross profit:** ₹1.50

**Gross margin:** 50%

This should be monitored as commercial costs are introduced.

---

### Distribution

Track:

* Number of wholesalers
* Number of retailers
* Number of institutions
* Geographic coverage

---

## 6. Customer Adoption KPIs

Once LETS enters the market, measure:

### Trial Rate

Percentage of customers who try LETS after encountering the product.

### Repeat Purchase Rate

Percentage of customers who purchase LETS again.

### Customer Satisfaction

Measure through a simple post-purchase rating.

### Recommendation

Track willingness of customers to recommend LETS to others.

---

## 7. Institutional KPIs

For schools and institutions:

* Number of institutional leads
* Number of institutional customers
* Average bulk-order size
* Repeat institutional orders
* Customization orders
* Institutional retention

---

## 8. KPI Hierarchy

### Product

**Writing Quality → Sharpening → Durability**

↓

### Operations

**Production Cost → Production Time → Defect Rate**

↓

### Commercial

**Gross Margin → Distribution → Units Sold**

↓

### Growth

**Repeat Purchases → Institutional Orders → Market Expansion**

## 10. Measurement Philosophy

The most important principle for LETS is:

> **Do not optimize cost at the expense of product quality.**

A lower production cost is only valuable if the product continues to meet the expected writing and durability standards.

Therefore, LETS should use a balanced product scorecard:

**Quality + Cost + Adoption + Commercial Viability**

---

## 11. PM Takeaway

The KPI framework turns the LETS product vision into measurable outcomes.

The immediate focus is:

> **Validate product quality → control unit economics → prove customer adoption → scale distribution.**

This creates a measurable path from prototype to commercial product.
