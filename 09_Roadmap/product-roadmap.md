# LETS — Product Roadmap

## 1. Roadmap Objective

The LETS roadmap is designed to move the product from a working prototype toward a scalable, commercially viable sustainable stationery product.

The roadmap follows:

> **Validate → Improve → Standardize → Launch → Scale**

---

## 2. Phase 1 — Prototype Validation

### Objective

Validate the fundamental product concept.

### Completed / Current Work

* Developed recycled-paper pencil prototype.
* Tested writing functionality.
* Tested compatibility with regular pencil sharpeners.
* Developed initial LETS branding/design.
* Established prototype-level production cost.

### Key Output

**Working recycled-paper pencil prototype**

---

## 3. Phase 2 — Product Optimization

### Objective

Improve consistency and product quality.

### Priorities

* Improve paper-body consistency.
* Improve lead alignment.
* Improve bonding.
* Improve finishing and surface quality.
* Test durability over extended use.
* Test multiple prototype samples.

### Success Criteria

Consistent product performance across multiple prototypes.

---

## 4. Phase 3 — Manufacturing Standardization

### Objective

Convert the prototype process into a repeatable manufacturing process.

### Priorities

* Standardize paper dimensions.
* Standardize paper rolling.
* Standardize lead placement.
* Standardize adhesive application.
* Standardize polishing.
* Define quality-control checkpoints.

### Success Criteria

Repeatable production with consistent dimensions and performance.

---

## 5. Phase 4 — Mechanization

### Objective

Increase production capacity while improving cost efficiency.

### Potentially Mechanized Processes

**Paper Rolling → Lead Integration → Polishing → Finishing → Branding**

### Expected Benefits

* Higher production capacity
* Lower manual effort
* Improved consistency
* Reduced production time
* Potentially lower unit cost

### Success Criteria

Demonstrate improved throughput without compromising product quality.

---

## 6. Phase 5 — Market Pilot

### Objective

Validate commercial demand before large-scale expansion.

### Initial Channels

* Selected stationery retailers
* Wholesalers
* Schools
* Colleges
* Educational institutions

### Test Variables

* Customer acceptance
* Wholesale pricing
* Retail pricing
* Product quality
* Retailer feedback
* Repeat purchases

### Success Criteria

Evidence of customer demand and viable unit economics.

---

## 7. Phase 6 — Institutional Growth

### Objective

Develop higher-volume B2B opportunities.

### Opportunities

* School-branded pencils
* College/event pencils
* Institutional bulk orders
* Customized packaging
* Sustainability campaigns

### Success Criteria

Repeat institutional orders and scalable customization.

---

## 8. Phase 7 — Mass-Market Expansion

### Objective

Expand LETS beyond the initial educational market.

### Potential Channels

* Stationery retailers
* E-commerce
* Direct-to-consumer
* Distributors
* Large retail networks

### Potential Product Expansion

* Different graphite grades
* Multiple designs
* Premium finishes
* Sustainable stationery bundles
* Additional recycled-paper stationery products

---

## 9. Roadmap Summary

| Phase | Focus                         | Key Outcome         |
| ----- | ----------------------------- | ------------------- |
| **1** | Prototype Validation          | Working product     |
| **2** | Product Optimization          | Improved quality    |
| **3** | Manufacturing Standardization | Repeatable process  |
| **4** | Mechanization                 | Scalable production |
| **5** | Market Pilot                  | Demand validation   |
| **6** | Institutional Growth          | B2B expansion       |
| **7** | Mass-Market Expansion         | Broader adoption    |

---

## 10. Prioritization Logic

The roadmap intentionally delays large-scale expansion until the core product and economics are validated.

The sequence is:

**Product works**
↓
**Product is consistent**
↓
**Production is scalable**
↓
**Economics are viable**
↓
**Market demand is validated**
↓
**Distribution expands**

This reduces the risk of scaling a product before its quality and economics are proven.

---

## 11. PM Takeaway

The roadmap treats LETS as a product that must evolve through measurable stages rather than attempting immediate mass-market launch.

> **First prove the product. Then prove the process. Then prove the market. Then scale.**
