# Market & Competitive Analysis

## 1. Market Context

LETS operates in the sustainable stationery space, specifically the emerging category of recycled-paper pencils.

The competitive landscape shows that recycled-paper pencils are already available in the Indian market. This means LETS needs to compete on more than sustainability alone.

The product opportunity is to combine:

> **Sustainability + Familiar Functionality + Affordability + Customization**

---

## 2. Competitive Landscape

### Competitor 1 — Greenr

Greenr offers recycled-paper and newspaper pencils in multiple pack sizes and formats. Its product range demonstrates that recycled-paper pencils can be positioned as everyday sustainable stationery.

Greenr's current listed products include recycled-paper pencils and newspaper pencils, with different pack configurations and price points.

### Competitor 2 — NotJustPencil

NotJustPencil focuses specifically on recycled-newspaper pencils and positions sustainability alongside social impact.

Its products use recycled newspapers/magazines, while the company also highlights rural women’s self-help groups and an education-focused give-back model.

The company also states that its pencils are designed to sharpen like regular wooden pencils, reinforcing the importance of maintaining familiar customer usage.

### Competitor 3 — Rescript

Rescript is a broader sustainable stationery brand offering recycled-paper stationery across categories including pencils, pens, notebooks and journals.

Its positioning focuses on making recycled paper part of everyday stationery rather than treating sustainability as a niche product attribute.

## 4. Competitive Insight

### Sustainability Alone Is Not Enough

Existing products already demonstrate that recycled-paper pencils can be positioned around sustainability.

Therefore, LETS should avoid a generic value proposition such as:

> “LETS is an eco-friendly pencil.”

Instead, LETS should aim for a broader proposition:

> **An affordable recycled-paper pencil designed to provide the familiarity of a conventional pencil while enabling scalable manufacturing and customization.**

---

## 5. LETS Differentiation Hypothesis

The initial differentiation strategy is built around four pillars:

### 1. Sustainability

Use recycled paper as the primary pencil-body material.

### 2. Familiar Functionality

The prototype can be sharpened using a standard pencil sharpener and provides a writing experience comparable to a conventional pencil.

### 3. Affordability

The current prototype-level production cost is ₹1.50 per pencil, creating a foundation for an affordable pricing strategy.

### 4. Customization

Future customization can create opportunities for schools, colleges, events and institutional bulk orders.

---

## 6. Competitive Positioning

LETS aims to position itself at the intersection of:

**Sustainable Product**

*

**Mass-Market Affordability**

*

**Conventional User Experience**

*

**Institutional Customization**

This positioning creates a broader product strategy than competing solely on environmental benefits.

---

## 7. Key Product Opportunity

The analysis suggests that the key opportunity for LETS is not simply to create another recycled-paper pencil.

The larger opportunity is:

> **Build a scalable sustainable stationery product that is affordable enough for everyday use and flexible enough for institutional and customized applications.**

---

## 8. Assumptions Requiring Validation

The following assumptions should be validated before commercial launch:

* Customers are willing to switch from conventional wooden pencils.
* ₹3.00 wholesale pricing can support a sustainable business after all operating costs are included.
* The ₹1.50 prototype production cost can be maintained or reduced at scale.
* Product quality remains consistent during mechanized production.
* Schools and institutions have sufficient demand for bulk/customized pencils.
* Customization creates enough additional value to justify its production complexity.

---

## 9. PM Takeaway

The competitive analysis changed the product strategy from:

> **“Make an eco-friendly pencil.”**

to:

> **“Make sustainable pencil adoption easy by combining environmental value with familiar functionality, affordability and scalable distribution.”**

This shift defines the core product strategy for LETS.
