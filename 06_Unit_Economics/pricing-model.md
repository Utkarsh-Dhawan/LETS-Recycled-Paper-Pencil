# Unit Economics & Pricing Model

## 1. Objective

The pricing strategy for LETS needs to balance three objectives:

1. Keep the product affordable for mass-market adoption.
2. Provide sufficient margin across the distribution chain.
3. Maintain product quality while reducing production cost through process optimization.

---

## 2. Current Cost & Pricing Structure

Based on the current prototype and proposed distribution model:

| Stage                     |    Price per Pencil |
| ------------------------- | ------------------: |
| Prototype production cost |           **₹1.50** |
| LETS → Wholesaler         |           **₹3.00** |
| Wholesaler → Retailer     |           **₹3.80** |
| Final consumer price      | **To be validated** |

---

## 3. LETS Gross Economics

### Production Cost

**₹1.50 per pencil**

### Wholesale Selling Price

**₹3.00 per pencil**

### Gross Profit

> ₹3.00 − ₹1.50 = **₹1.50 per pencil**

### Gross Margin

> ₹1.50 ÷ ₹3.00 × 100 = **50%**

Therefore, at the current prototype cost and proposed wholesale price, LETS has a **50% gross margin before additional commercial expenses**.

---

## 4. Distribution Economics

The proposed distribution chain is:

**LETS → Wholesaler → Retailer → Customer**

Current price assumptions:

| Participant |        Purchase Price |
| ----------- | --------------------: |
| LETS        | ₹1.50 production cost |
| Wholesaler  |                 ₹3.00 |
| Retailer    |                 ₹3.80 |
| Customer    |      To be determined |

The ₹0.80 difference between the wholesaler and retailer purchase prices provides room for retailer economics, while the final consumer price will need to be determined through market validation.

---

## 5. Cost Optimization Strategy

The current ₹1.50 cost represents prototype-level production.

The long-term objective is to improve the cost structure through:

### Process Standardization

Standardize:

* Paper dimensions
* Paper rolling
* Lead placement
* Adhesive application
* Finishing
* Branding

### Mechanization

Introduce machine-assisted processes for repetitive manufacturing activities.

Potential benefits:

* Higher production capacity
* Lower manual effort
* More consistent quality
* Reduced production time
* Lower unit cost at scale

## 7. Pricing Strategy

LETS is intended to follow an **affordability-led pricing strategy**.

The product should remain accessible to students and everyday stationery users while preserving sufficient margin for the distribution network.

The initial wholesale price assumption is:

> **₹3.00 per pencil**

The final retail price should be validated using:

* Competitor pricing
* Customer willingness to pay
* Retailer margin requirements
* Distribution costs
* Packaging costs
* Scaled manufacturing costs

---

## 8. Key Business Trade-off

The central economic challenge is:

> **Reduce production cost without reducing product quality.**

This creates a product optimization equation:

**Lower Cost + Consistent Quality + Sustainable Material = Scalable Product**

---

## 9. Economics at Scale

The long-term objective is not simply to increase the selling price.

Instead, LETS should aim to improve economics by increasing manufacturing efficiency.

### Current State

Prototype production
→ Higher manual effort
→ ₹1.50 estimated/current prototype cost

### Future State

Standardized + mechanized production
→ Higher throughput
→ Lower wastage
→ Improved consistency
→ Potentially lower unit cost

---

## 10. Economic Risks

Before commercial launch, the following assumptions need validation:

* Whether ₹1.50 production cost can be maintained at larger volumes.
* Whether machinery investment improves unit economics sufficiently.
* Whether packaging and logistics materially increase cost.
* Whether the ₹3.00 wholesale price remains competitive.
* Whether retailers can achieve attractive margins at the final consumer price.
* Whether product quality remains consistent as production scales.

---

## 11. PM Takeaway

The pricing strategy connects product design with business viability.

The key product decision is to **optimize the manufacturing process rather than compromise the customer experience**.

> **The goal is not simply to make LETS cheaper. The goal is to make LETS cheaper to produce while keeping the product experience comparable to a conventional pencil.**
