# MVP & Feature Prioritization

## 1. MVP Objective

The goal of the LETS MVP is to validate whether a recycled-paper pencil can deliver the core experience expected from a conventional pencil while remaining practical and affordable.

The MVP therefore focuses on the product attributes that are essential to adoption before investing in advanced customization or product variants.

---

## 2. MVP Definition

### Core MVP

> **A recycled-paper pencil that writes like a conventional pencil, can be sharpened using a standard sharpener, and can be produced at an affordable cost.**

The MVP prioritizes functionality and reliability over additional features.

## 4. Prioritization Framework

LETS uses a simple **Must Have / Should Have / Could Have** framework.

### Must Have

Features required for the product to function and solve the core problem.

* Recycled-paper body
* Functional graphite core
* Good writing performance
* Durability
* Standard sharpening
* Affordable production

### Should Have

Features that improve the product experience or commercial potential but are not required for the first functional MVP.

* LETS branding
* Attractive design
* Sustainable packaging
* Institutional customization

### Could Have

Features that can be introduced after the core product has been validated.

* Personalized designs
* Multiple pencil variants
* Customized packaging
* Expanded stationery products

---

## 5. Product Trade-offs

The key product trade-off is:

> **Cost vs. Quality**

Reducing manufacturing cost should not compromise the writing performance or durability expected from a conventional pencil.

A second trade-off is:

> **Customization vs. Manufacturing Complexity**

Customization can increase the product's appeal for institutional customers, but excessive customization may increase production complexity and cost.

Therefore, customization is positioned as a secondary capability rather than an MVP requirement.

---

## 6. Product Requirements

### Functional Requirements

1. Pencil must use recycled paper as its primary body material.
2. Pencil must contain a functional graphite writing core.
3. Pencil should provide writing performance comparable to a conventional pencil.
4. Pencil should be compatible with a standard pencil sharpener.
5. Pencil should withstand normal student usage.

### Business Requirements

1. Production should target an affordable unit cost.
2. Product should support wholesale and retail distribution.
3. Product should be suitable for institutional bulk orders.
4. Manufacturing should be capable of future process standardization and mechanization.

---

## 7. MVP Success Criteria

The MVP will be considered successful when:

* The pencil provides acceptable everyday writing performance.
* The pencil can be sharpened using a standard sharpener.
* Product construction is sufficiently durable for normal use.
* Prototype-level economics can be translated into a scalable cost model.
* The manufacturing process can be standardized for consistent output.

---

## 8. Future Product Expansion

After validating the MVP, LETS can expand into:

### Institutional Products

* School-branded pencils
* College/event pencils
* Bulk customized orders

### Consumer Products

* Multiple designs
* Different grades of graphite
* Premium finishes
* Sustainable stationery bundles

### Long-Term Product Portfolio

The manufacturing platform could eventually support additional recycled-paper stationery products beyond pencils.

---

## 9. PM Decision

The product strategy is to **solve the core problem before expanding the feature set**.

> **First make the pencil work exceptionally well. Then make it scalable. Then make it customizable.**

This sequencing reduces product complexity while allowing LETS to progressively expand its market opportunity.
