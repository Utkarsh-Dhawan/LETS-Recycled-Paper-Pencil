# Problem Statement & Product Opportunity

## 1. Background

Pencils are a simple, familiar and widely used stationery product. Their everyday use makes them an interesting opportunity for sustainable product innovation.

LETS explores an alternative to the conventional wooden pencil by using recycled paper as the primary body material.

The objective is not simply to create an environmentally friendly pencil. The larger challenge is to make the sustainable alternative practical, affordable and familiar enough for everyday use.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

> **How might we create a recycled-paper pencil that delivers the functionality and user experience of a conventional pencil while remaining affordable and scalable for mass adoption?**

The product needs to balance four key requirements:

* **Sustainability** — use recycled paper as the primary body material.
* **Quality** — maintain a writing experience comparable to a conventional pencil.
* **Affordability** — achieve a cost structure suitable for students and mass-market consumers.
* **Scalability** — develop a manufacturing process that can eventually support higher production volumes.

---

## 3. Product Opportunity

LETS identifies an opportunity to combine sustainable materials with the familiarity of an everyday stationery product.

Instead of asking customers to adopt an entirely new product category, LETS aims to preserve the familiar pencil experience while changing the underlying body material.

### Core Product Hypothesis

> **If a recycled-paper pencil can provide comparable writing performance, work with a standard sharpener and remain affordable, customers may have little reason to change their existing pencil usage habits.**

---

## 4. Target Customer Segments

### Initial Segments

**School Students**

Students represent a high-frequency stationery user group where affordability and everyday functionality are important.

**College Students**

College students provide an additional consumer segment and potential early adopters of sustainability-focused products.

**Schools & Educational Institutions**

Institutions represent a potential B2B channel for bulk purchases, sustainability initiatives and customized stationery.

### Future Segments

* Parents and general consumers
* Stationery retailers
* Corporate/event customers
* Bulk institutional buyers
* Customized stationery customers

---

## 5. Product Goals

### Primary Goal

Develop a recycled-paper pencil that maintains conventional-pencil functionality while improving the sustainability of the product body.

### Secondary Goals

1. Maintain compatibility with standard pencil sharpeners.
2. Develop an affordable production model.
3. Improve consistency through standardized manufacturing.
4. Explore mechanization of repetitive production steps.
5. Enable customized branding for institutional and bulk orders.
6. Build a distribution model through wholesalers and retailers.

---

## 6. Key Product Constraints

The product must balance several trade-offs:

| Constraint     | Product Requirement                   |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Sustainability | Recycled-paper body                   |
| Quality        | Comparable writing performance        |
| Usability      | Standard-sharpener compatibility      |
| Cost           | Target prototype cost of ₹1.50/pencil |
| Scalability    | Standardized manufacturing process    |
| Design         | Attractive and customizable branding  |

---

## 7. Product Success Definition

At the prototype stage, LETS demonstrates two important functional characteristics:

* The pencil provides a writing experience comparable to a conventional pencil.
* The pencil can be sharpened using a regular pencil sharpener.

The next product-development challenge is to improve manufacturing consistency and determine whether the product economics remain viable when production is scaled.

---

## 8. Product Opportunity Summary

LETS is therefore positioned as more than an eco-friendly stationery product.

The product opportunity is to create a **sustainable, familiar and affordable everyday pencil** supported by a scalable manufacturing and distribution model.

**Product Principle:**

> **Change the material, not the everyday experience.**
